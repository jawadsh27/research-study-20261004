Thank you for participating in my research project.

You will be given two tasks where you can review Spanish words in two durations — 30 seconds and 60 seconds. Your first task may be either the 30-second or the 60-second one. After each review period, you will drag and drop the words to match them to their meanings.

Before you begin, please read the following guidelines carefully:

  • Please make sure you are not doing this right after you wake up or right before you go to bed.
  • Make sure there are no distractions around you during the task.
  • Please do not take any notes or ask for any help.

The purpose of this study is not to test your ability to learn Spanish words — it is about how you organize and process information.
